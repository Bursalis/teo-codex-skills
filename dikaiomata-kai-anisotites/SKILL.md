### Mandatory negative-construction gate

Before finalising every written deliverable, run the `teo-writing-academic` negative-construction and positive-development audit in full. This is a hard quality gate for critical summaries, the final lecture script, the outline, slide-plan wording and all PPTX text.

Inspect every sentence containing a negative or contrastive construction, including `δεν είναι`, `δεν σημαίνει`, `δεν δηλώνει`, `δεν ζητά`, `δεν αρκεί`, `δεν βρίσκεται`, `το πρόβλημα δεν είναι`, `όχι μόνο`, `δεν ... αλλά`, and grammatical variants. For every candidate, record internally: the sentence, the precise misreading or theoretical boundary that the negative clause prevents, and the revision decision.

Retain a negative clause only when it excludes a specific, plausible and consequential misreading of the verified argument. When it is retained, integrate it with the positive claim, its mechanism, condition or evidentiary basis in one wider syntactic and analytical movement. Never preserve a standalone denial followed by a separate affirmative sentence, or a sequential variant such as «δεν ζητά ...· υποστηρίζει ...», «δεν παρουσιάζεται ως ...· συνδέεται με ...», «η διαφορά δεν βρίσκεται ...· βρίσκεται ...» or «το πρόβλημα δεν είναι ...· το πρόβλημα είναι ...».

If the negative clause adds only cadence, apparent nuance, an introduction, rhetorical contrast or word-count length, rewrite from the positive proposition. A deliverable fails this gate until every retained negative construction has a determinate argumentative function and no positive claim has been unnecessarily split into a denial and an assertion.

In the ordinary flow of prose, lead with the claim, mechanism or evidence, then place the source in an APA author–date parenthetical citation at the end of the relevant sentence or clause. Do not make the author of an individual article or chapter the grammatical subject merely to introduce an argument. Thus write «Οι ασιατικές μητροπόλεις και εμπορικές πόλεις έχουν μακρές, ετερογενείς ιστορίες (Machimura, 2024)», not «Ο Machimura επισημαίνει ότι …».

Use a scholar's name in the prose only when it is analytically necessary: to identify a foundational figure whose intervention organises the field (for example Simmel, Weber, Lefebvre, Harvey or Bourdieu), to name a theory or school of thought, or to stage an explicit comparison between competing positions in a debate. When an author is associated with a school, name the school rather than using the individual as a rhetorical proxy: write «Η Σχολή του Λος Άντζελες … (Dear, 2013)», not «Ο Dear …». Apply this rule to all prose, including critical summaries, lecture scripts, outlines, slides and captions.

Use Greek throughout. Explain technical terms at first use. Write for final-year undergraduates: theoretically accurate, clear and pedagogical without unnecessary abstraction. Keep political stakes analytically visible but never substitute slogans for mechanisms.

### Recovery and completion protocol

Treat each quality check as a phase gate, not a reason to abandon the production run. When a check fails, identify the earliest phase that can correct the failure, return to that phase, repair the cause, regenerate every dependent artifact affected by the repair and rerun the relevant gates. Examples: a failed critical-summary word, duplicate or source-fidelity audit returns to that reading and its `critical-paper-summary` synthesis; a duplicate-prose or semantic-structure failure returns to the argument dossier and requires a new complete script, never a local deletion or patch; uncertain evidence returns to source reading and the evidence map; an out-of-range or analytically incomplete script returns to the argument dossier redesign gate; an unclear opening or missing section transitions returns to the script; an outline that does not clearly explain section content and relationships returns to the outline and, if needed, the dossier and script; unfaithful or fragmented slide wording returns to the slide plan; layout defects return to the deck builder; broken exports return to the isolated build environment and preflight.

In the initial phase, do not claim completion or deliver the three written files while any of their required gates fails; continue repair until all three are validated. After delivering them, stop and wait for the user's review. In the second phase, do not deliver the PPTX until its gates and the complete package audit pass. Do not substitute prior outputs, uncited material or degraded artifacts to bypass a failed gate.
